# Code of conduct

AgniNetra adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than
people, and remember that the subject, fires and the air people breathe, affects real
communities and real livelihoods. Do not use the project's data to single out a farmer,
a village or a person.

Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not
accepted anywhere in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and the
community labelling in Live.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory from the Security tab, or contact
the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in
confidence. The maintainer may remove content and block accounts that break this code.
